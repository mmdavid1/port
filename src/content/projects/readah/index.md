## What is it

Reading app designed to have a social element to reading.

## Why I'm building it

I read a lot but found that it was hard to find friends who were reading or had read the same book I was around the same time period, so we couldn't discuss the book that well. It would be cool if you could see other people's thoughts as you are going through a book.

Check it out at [readah.app](https://www.readah.app/).
